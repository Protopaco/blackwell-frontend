# [057] Salary calculation

**Board status:** Backlog
**Part of:** [048] Expand EmployeeActivityRates (Epic)
**Depends on:** [056]

## Background

**This is `blackwell-backend` work.**

Decided in epic discussion: a salaried employee's `salaryAmount` (on Employee, see [051]) is a lump sum per pay period, distributed across whichever activities they logged as `payRateType: salary` that period — the opposite of hourly math (rate ÷ hours instead of hours × rate).

- `effectiveHourlyRate = employee.salaryAmount / (sum of hours logged against that employee's salary-type bridge rows, for that pay period)`
- Each salary-type activity's dollar allocation = `hoursInThatActivity × effectiveHourlyRate`
- Hourly/flat-rate activities for the same employee calculate independently via [056] and are additive on top (e.g. a salaried employee's paid on-call shift) — not folded into the salary divisor
- This can only be computed once actual hours are known, i.e. at allocation/report time (`buildAllocationRows`), same point [056] runs — not at timesheet-generation time

## Scope

- Extend `buildAllocationRows`/`resolveDollarRate` (or a new sibling function, whichever fits better once [056] lands) to compute the per-pay-period effective hourly rate for each salaried employee and apply it to their salary-type activity rows
- Confirm the zero-salary-hours case is fully handled by [058]'s validation (this ticket shouldn't need its own fallback/guard if [058] blocks report generation first)
