# [037] Remove pay period status chip from pay period creation dialog

**Board status:** Backlog

## Background

`CreatePayPeriodDialog.tsx` shows a `PayPeriodStatusChip` next to the suggested next pay period's name (`suggested.status`). Since the suggested period hasn't been created yet, its status is always whatever a brand-new pay period defaults to — the chip conveys no information here and just adds visual noise to the creation flow.

## Scope

Remove the `PayPeriodStatusChip` from `CreatePayPeriodDialog.tsx`'s suggested-pay-period display. Keep the name and date range.
